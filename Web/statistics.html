Column definitions:   Taxa tracked — all species/genera assessed in this dashboard.   Present (global) — taxa with barcodes in BOTH NCBI AND BOLD/SILVA globally; detectable by eDNA but records may not be from Nigeria.   Partial (WA gap) — sequences in one database only, or lacking West African / Nigerian records; unreliable for local surveys.   Absent (0 globally) — zero sequences anywhere on Earth; highest priority for new barcoding.   Global coverage % — percentage of taxa with at least one global barcode record. Note: true Nigerian coverage is near-zero for most commercially important coastal taxa.
