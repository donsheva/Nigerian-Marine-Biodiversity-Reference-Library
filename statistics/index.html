Statistics

Live database coverage statistics for the Nigerian Marine Biodiversity Reference Library. Coverage is computed dynamically from the data arrays — all stats update automatically when classifications change. Present requires West African records meeting the marker threshold (COI ≥5, 12S ≥3, 18S ≥1, 28S ≥1, rbcL ≥3) in both NCBI and BOLD/SILVA. Absent = zero WA records in both databases. Coverage reflects regional (West African) reference availability, not global sequence count.

The conservation-urgency view: IUCN-listed threatened species (Vulnerable, Endangered, Critically Endangered) cross-tabulated against reference coverage. Threatened taxa with absent or partial coverage cannot be reliably monitored by eDNA — a gap with direct consequences for fisheries policy, MPA management, and CITES reporting.

Column definitions:   Taxa tracked — all species/genera assessed in this dashboard.   Present (regional) — taxa with qualifying barcodes in BOTH NCBI AND BOLD/SILVA, with WA records meeting the marker threshold (COI ≥5, 12S ≥3, 18S ≥1, 28S ≥1, rbcL ≥3).   Partial (regional gap) — WA records exist but fall below marker threshold, or only one database covered.   Absent (WA gap) — zero West African records in both NCBI and BOLD/SILVA; highest priority for new barcoding regardless of global availability.   % Fully present — percentage of taxa meeting the full PRESENT threshold (WA records in both NCBI and BOLD/SILVA). Partial taxa (some data but below threshold) are not counted here — see the Partial column.
